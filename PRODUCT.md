# PRODUCT.md — GrantAI Product Identity

## Overview
GrantAI is the world's first fully autonomous, audit-proof R&D Tax Credit Engine. It replaces legacy 25% success-fee consultants with a high-trust, fixed-cost SaaS platform. It analyzes engineering metadata (GitHub, Jira) using a two-pass compliance engine (Frascati Manual standards) and routes calculations through a deterministic math engine to generate mathematically perfect, audit-ready claims.

## Register
`product` (Design serves the product by instilling absolute trust, clarity, and mathematical precision.)

## Target Users & Scene
- **User**: A stressed CTO, lead engineer, or CFO at a fast-growing tech startup.
- **Scene**: Filing technical justifications and checking financial calculations at 4 PM in a dimly lit office. They are tired of filling out endless surveys with accountants. They need speed, total compliance, and absolute clarity.
- **Tone**: Professional, precise, authoritative, dry, technically rigorous. No marketing fluff, no hype.

## Anti-References & Avoided Reflexes
- **No SaaS-Cream/AI-Purple**: No generic purple/indigo grids, neon glows, or fuzzy glassmorphic cards.
- **No "AI-Slop" Clichés**: Avoid identical cards in grids with Lucide icons on top, big gradient numbers with tiny labels underneath, and floating background blobs.
- **No Em Dashes**: Clean, precise punctuation.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Mathematical Authority**: Financial calculations and data tables should look precise, structured, and authentic, leveraging clean monospaced layout elements.
2. **Deterministic Flow**: The user must immediately see which steps are deterministic (rules, tax laws, calculations) and which are cognitive assistance (the Two-Pass compliance narrative generator).
3. **Audit-Ready Clarity**: High visual contrast (WCAG AA compliant) and typography hierarchy that facilitates immediate scanning.
