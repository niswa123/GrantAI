1. **The "Big 4" Tax Tax:** Companies outsource claims to legacy accounting firms. These firms charge **15% to 25%** of the *total recovered tax credit* just to fill out paperwork.

### How it Works (The Moat)

This isn't a simple "ChatGPT wrapper". We've engineered a proprietary **Two-Pass AI Engine paired with a Deterministic Math Calculator**.

*   **Pass 1: The Skeptical Auditor (Chain-of-Thought)**
    Before generating any text, our AI is forced to reason step-by-step. It identifies the industry baseline, pinpoints the exact technical advance, and evaluates the methodology. If the project lacks *genuine technical uncertainty*, the engine rejects it. This prevents "hallucinations" and protects the client from audit penalties.
*   **The Deterministic Core (Zero AI Math)**
    Tax laws are not suggestions; they are formulas. GrantAI routes the approved projects through a hard-coded, math-only rules engine specific to the user's jurisdiction (e.g., automatically applying the French 43% overhead *forfait*, or the German €2M salary cap).
*   **Pass 2: The Technical Writer**
    Finally, the approved metadata is synthesized into a dry, highly technical, third-person defense narrative perfectly tuned for tax authority review. Zero marketing fluff.
